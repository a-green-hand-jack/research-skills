# Literature Review

Turn a collection of papers into a structured account of what is known, how it is known, where studies disagree, and what remains unresolved.

## Core behavior

- Start from a defined question and audience.
- Make the search traceable rather than ad hoc.
- Take structured notes while reading.
- Compare evidence, not just conclusions.
- Organize around ideas, mechanisms, methods, or disputes rather than paper order.
- Treat a research gap as an unresolved scientific problem, not merely a low citation count.
