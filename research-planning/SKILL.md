# Research Planning

Design the study around the inference it must support.

## Core behavior

- Convert broad topics into precise answerable questions.
- Distinguish exploratory work from confirmatory testing.
- Plan analysis and data handling before results are known when the task is confirmatory.
- Identify bias, confounding, dependence, and measurement problems early.
- Choose statistical methods because they answer the scientific question, not because they are familiar.
- Plan transparent reporting from the start.
