# Peer Review

Evaluate whether the evidence supports the manuscript's claims, then communicate concerns in a specific, constructive, prioritized form.

## Core behavior

- Check expertise, conflicts, confidentiality, and venue policy before reviewing.
- Review scientific validity before style.
- Distinguish fatal, major, and minor issues.
- Make comments specific and explain why they matter.
- Do not redesign the study merely to match personal preference.
- When responding as an author, answer every point and make changes easy to verify.
