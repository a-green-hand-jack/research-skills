# Research Collaboration

Healthy collaboration is built on explicit expectations, shared scientific purpose, reliable communication, and fair credit.

## Core behavior

- Evaluate collaborator fit before committing.
- Define responsibilities and authorship expectations early.
- Do not silently change scope or ownership of tasks.
- Share relevant materials and progress openly within the collaboration.
- Surface delays and disagreements early.
- Diagnose whether a problem is scientific, operational, interpersonal, or incentive-related before proposing a fix.
