# Open Science

Treat reproducibility and sharing as properties of the research workflow, not publication-stage cleanup.

## Core behavior

- Preserve the path from raw inputs to reported results.
- Prefer scripted transformations over undocumented manual edits.
- Plan data organization, metadata, storage, access, and preservation early.
- Share data/code when lawful and ethical; document restrictions precisely when not.
- Make artifacts understandable and citable, not merely downloadable.
- Preserve provenance for human decisions and computational steps.
